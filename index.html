Matched Acoustic-Realization Evaluation Materials

This author-neutral data card exposes every reference waveform and every text/reference assignment in the 22-group counterfactual evaluation. It is an evaluation release, not a model or training-data release.

Open the complete three-system qualitative listener (all groups and seeds).

Open the speed, pitch, and volume 2×2 listener.

Scope. Each group crosses text endpoint A/B with reference endpoint A/B. The two reference waveforms below are the exact 5-second, RMS-matched signals used in all three systems. AC-T2A generation receives text and reference audio only; it never receives the anchor video.

Construction and safeguards

  • 30 candidate endpoint groups were frozen before AC-T2A generation; 22 passed the predeclared duration (≥7.9 s) and endpoint-distance (CLAP ≥0.15) checks.
  • Endpoint A/B audio was rendered with text-guided MMAudio while fixing one held-out VGGSound anchor and seed inside each group. Original videos and raw VGGSound audio are not redistributed.
  • Some A/B endpoints differ in source identity. The experiment measures matched condition allocation, not pure timbre transfer or perceptual quality.
  • Metadata, cell assignments, filenames, byte counts, and SHA-256 hashes are available under metadata/.
